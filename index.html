Dev log · November 2026

25 Nov · Rider

A whole new rider

Your rider got rebuilt from the boots up: real-world proportions, a glossy down puffer with fat baffles and a storm flap, ribbed ankle cuffs, real ski boots with buckles, padded gloves that grip your poles, plus new goggles with a mirrored lens, a proper helmet, knit beanies and a fluffy pom.

25 Nov · Feature

First-person mode

Press V while riding, or pick Camera in the menu, to ride from inside your goggles. Move your mouse to look around, and the view turns with your head through every spin and flip, so corks feel wild. Press V again to jump back to third person.

24 Nov · Lobby

A lobby of our own

Brand-new lobby cards. Your rider card is now a season lift pass with trail markers that light up as you set a best on each park, plus a daily challenge card and a big Drop in button.

24 Nov · Rider

Quilted puffers

Outfits got a full rebuild: boxy quilted puffer jackets with a chest band that runs across the sleeves, a bigger hood, chunky gloves and baggy pants that bunch over your boots. Every Locker colour works with it.

24 Nov · Snow

Carved tracks

Skis now leave two thin grooves in the corduroy and boards leave one wide one. Carve hard and they dig deeper. Fixed a pale flickering strip some graphics cards showed behind the rider.

24 Nov · Patch

The big flaw hunt

17 fixes in one go: no more pyramid mountains at the finish, proper crash tumbles, smoother autoland, a lighter terrain for faster frame rates, and a full pass on phone layouts.

23 Nov · Parks

Bigger jumps, wider parks

Every table grew: longer ramps, bigger decks, longer landings and roughly two seconds of air. Rows are spaced further apart and the trail is now about 100 m wide.

23 Nov · Feature

Night mode

Press N (or open the menu) to switch between day, sunset, blue hour and night. Night brings a moon, stars, more lamps and warm pools of light on the snow.

23 Nov · Tricks

New trick animation

Your hands really reach the skis on grabs, shoulders lead every spin, knees tuck on flips and you open up to stomp the landing. Spins are slower and pop is softer for better control.

Thanks for riding. More parks, more tricks and more gear are on the way. — the Freshline team

Keys
A / D
Carve on snow, spin in the air. Hold with S for a cork, with W for a bio, or with a roll key for a flatspin.
W
Pole and tuck on snow. Frontflip in the air.
S
Brake on snow. Backflip in the air.
← → / J L
Roll in the air. Alone it's a sideflip.
↓ / K
Chill: hold it off the lip for a half-speed air.
Space
Hold to load, release to pop. A loaded pop spins and flips faster.
Shift Q E
Mute, tail and Japan grabs. Hold 0.25 s to count, 1 s for the full bonus.
B
Butter: hold B and press A or D on the snow.
On a rail
A / D swaps your slide, Space hops. Hold a spin key at the end to spin off.
R · Esc · H · N
Restart · menu · hide key hints · change time of day (day, sunset, blue hour, night).
V
Switch between third-person and first-person view. In first person, move the mouse to look around.

Scoring
Difficulty
2,000 × turns²: 360 = 2,000, 720 = 8,000, 1080 = 18,000. +15% each time you go upside down, ×1.25 off switch.
Amplitude
180 a second in the air and 48 a metre of height. Small hops off flat snow don't count.
Grabs
Count after 0.25 s. Up to +25% of the rotation (all of it when held 1 s), +5% for each extra grab, plus 1,120 a second and 400 per grab.
Variety
Your last 3 tricks are remembered. Repeats pay 70%, 45%, then 30%. Left and right, switch and on/off a rail are different tricks.
Flow
A line is everything from leaving the snow to landing on it again. Each extra link (air, rail, air) adds ×0.25.
Landing
Stomped (within 17°, over your skis): +600 and 15% of the line. Saved it: 85% down to 50% fully sideways. Crash: the line is gone.
Rails · butters
1,000 a second on the bar and 600 per swap or hop. Butters: press 200, 180 = 400, 360 = 800, first 4 of a run.
